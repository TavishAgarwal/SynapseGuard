SYNAPSEGUARD PART A STATISTICAL SUMMARY & HYPOTHESIS EVALUATION

Hypothesis H1 Evaluation: PARTIALLY HELD

1. Summary Statement:
   Hypothesis H1 ("Next-token predictability positively correlates with SAE 
   latent sparsity across input categories") partially held. Across all 
   categories combined, both models demonstrated statistically significant 
   positive correlations between output logit predictability and SAE latent 
   sparsity (Gemma-2-2B: Pearson r = 0.29–0.30, p < 0.001; GPT-2: Pearson 
   r = 0.39–0.65, p < 0.001).

2. Per-Category Breakdown:
   - Constrained Factual: H1 HELD STRONGLY (Gemma r = 0.61, GPT-2 r = 0.86, p < 0.001)
   - Adversarial / Ambiguous: H1 HELD STRONGLY (Gemma r = 0.68, GPT-2 r = 0.82, p < 0.001)
   - Moderate Reasoning: H1 HELD MODERATELY (Gemma r = 0.46, GPT-2 r = 0.77, p < 0.001)
   - Open-Ended: H1 DID NOT HOLD on Gemma-2-2B (Gemma r = 0.09–0.10, p > 0.05, 
     not significant); H1 held on GPT-2 (r = 0.50–0.70, p < 0.001).

3. Scientific Interpretation:
   For structured, factual, and reasoning tasks, low next-token entropy (high 
   predictability) corresponds directly to higher SAE latent sparsity (fewer 
   active feature latents). However, for open-ended creative generation, 
   output logit entropy fluctuates without a corresponding increase in active 
   SAE latents on frontier-architecture models like Gemma-2-2B.
